Geelong’s trusted name in funeral care

For more than 140 years, Tuckers Funeral & Bereavement Service has been part of Geelong's fabric, guiding local families through life's most difficult moments. Our experienced funeral directors provide a compassionate, seamless service that honours your loved one's wishes.

A legacy of local care

We’ve been proudly supporting Geelong families for over 140 years.

Every farewell is personal

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all funerals. Our team takes the time to understand your loved one's story so we can create a service that truly reflects their life.

Giving back to our community

Every year we invest in Geelong organisations, clubs, charities and events to support our community.

Continued care

We provide ongoing bereavement support to help families as they heal including our yearly Time to Remember Christmas service.
