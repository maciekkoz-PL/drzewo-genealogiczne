What are BillionGraves Headstone Records?

BillionGraves Headstone Records contain photos of gravestones taken by volunteers in cemeteries around the world. The names, dates, and other information have been transcribed and are searchable.

What genealogical data can I learn from BillionGraves Headstone Records?

The BillionGraves Headstone Records may include:

First name
Last name
Age at Death
Spouse
Gender
Occupation
Names and relationships of family members
Clubs, hobbies, organizations, and interests
Religion
Burial Location and Cemetery
Military Information
Birthplace or Country of Origin
What years does it cover?

The BillionGraves Headstone Records span from about 1600 to the current day.
