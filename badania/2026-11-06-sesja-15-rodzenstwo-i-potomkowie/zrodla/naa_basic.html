Session time out

You are logged in as guest

You are no longer in an active session, this can happen when:

  • There is no activity on the screen for more than 30 minutes
  • a link or bookmark referred to an expired session
  • RecordSearch has experienced an outage
  • browser preferences were changed, eg. disabled cookies. RecordSearch requires cookies to be enabled to store a session identifier in your browser's memory.

To start an active session, you can search as a guest
