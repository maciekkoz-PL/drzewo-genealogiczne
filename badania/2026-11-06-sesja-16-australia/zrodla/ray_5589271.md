CHMIELEWSKI, Raymond (Chima)

Ray Chmielewski formerly of Geelong Victoria, passed away with family by his side on the 19th November 2020. Deeply loved husband of Rose and much loved Father and Father-in-Law of Michelle and Chris, Simone and Nikki, Nicole and Dean. Very much loved Opa of Rebecca, Robert, Max, Ebony, Tori, Marcus and Stella. Loved Great Opa to their children. Always in our hearts and loved forever. Ray's funeral service will be held at Allambe Memorial Park, 129 Nerang-Broadbeach Road, Nerang QLD 4211 on Friday the 27th November 2020 at 10am
